Improved Tea Harvesting Machine Project

Project Overview
This repository contains the documentation, research, and design files for our 3rd-year Mechanical Engineering project. Our objective is to modify and improve existing tea plucking machinery to enable the collection of premium tea products.

Project Log & Milestones

1. Field Research & Site Visits
* Conducted a site visit to the Tea Research Centre in Ratnapura.
* Observed current harvesting methods and identified practical field constraints.

2. Literature Review
* Analyzed existing research papers regarding tea harvesting mechanisms.
* Extracted key data to form the theoretical foundation of our modifications.

3. Conceptualization & Supervisor Meetings
* Conducted brainstorming and feedback sessions with our project supervisor.
* Developed initial mechanical concepts and design directions based on our field research and literature review.
